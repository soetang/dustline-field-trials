Dustline Native asset provenance

Original code, shaders, map geometry, UI, generated audio and Blender models:
MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 Dustline: Field Trials contributors. See ../LICENSE.
The native 0.4 operator generator is native-godot/tools/make_operators.py.
The four first-person weapon models remain copies generated by the repository's
scripts/make-weapons.py. No Valve models, maps or recordings are used.

The six 1024x1024 concrete surface JPEGs are Poly Haven CC0-1.0 assets:
https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor
https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_wall_001
https://polyhaven.com/license
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

These are unchanged copies of the already-documented textures in this
repository's assets/textures directory. Exact download URLs, byte sizes and
MD5 checksums are included in native-godot/assets/textures/sources.json.
They supply diffuse colour, OpenGL normals and ambient-occlusion/roughness maps.
CC0 textures remain CC0; they are not claimed as our original MIT artwork.

Godot and its open-source dependencies/font: GODOT-LICENSE.txt and
GODOT-COPYRIGHT.txt in this directory. No proprietary plugins, asset packs,
commercial audio or image-generator outputs are bundled.
