DUSTLINE NATIVE: COURTYARD — 0.3
Experimental Sol + Astra run. Open-source, single-player tactical prototype.
An original Dust2-inspired map, not the Valve map or an affiliated product.

WINDOWS: Run DustlineNative.exe. Keep DustlineNative.pck beside it.
If Vulkan has trouble, run Compatibility mode.cmd (OpenGL).
LINUX: Run ./DustlineNative.x86_64 with its .pck beside it.
No engine editor, browser, Epic account or internet connection is required.

Enter / Deploy starts the seven-second buy phase. Nobody can move or shoot
until ROUND LIVE. You are CT: defend the sites and hold E to defuse the device.
WASD move, mouse look, left click fire, right click aim/scope, R reload.
Space jump, Shift walk, Ctrl crouch, B armory, 1-4 buy, Tab scoreboard.
Escape pause, F11 fullscreen, F3 diagnostics.
After death: follow living teammates. Left click / Space selects next;
right click selects previous. Tab recalls the damage recap after it fades.
F8 saves a screenshot without using an external screenshot shortcut.
Pause > Open screenshots folder finds your captures.
Pause > Copy feedback details copies the build, FPS, position and controls state.
Nothing is uploaded automatically. Audio is synthesized noise and tones.

This is an early native foundation, not CS2-quality finished artwork or a
competitive multiplayer release. Four weapons; no grenades or networking yet.
